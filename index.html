Skipping tips

  • Jump low, just enough for the rope to pass. Land softly on the balls of your feet.
  • Check your ceiling height and clear some floor space first.
  • No room for the rope? Skip with an imaginary rope. It works just as well.
  • Short rounds with rests are perfect. You don’t need to go non-stop.
  • A mat or rug is kinder to your knees.
